# Pastel Pockets PROD Demo v0.1.0

**Version:** v0.1.0
**Status:** Pre-Release
**Platform:** Android

## Summary

The first public Pastel Pockets PROD demo provides core personal finance
tracking and a preview of AI-assisted insights. This is an evaluation build,
not a finished financial advice service.

## Highlights

- Record and review income and expenses.
- Manage wallets, budgets, and savings goals.
- View spending reports and filter transaction history.
- Import and export transactions using CSV.
- Choose Guest access or Google sign-in.
- Explore AI-assisted financial insights and forecasts.

## In development

Receipt scanning/OCR and the conversational AI chatbot are marked as coming
soon in the PROD app. Split Bill is not offered as an available feature in this
demo. Further work will be guided by feedback; no delivery dates are promised.

## Known limitations

- Pre-release demo; features and behavior may change.
- Android only.
- AI insights are informational and may not be suitable for every financial
  decision. Verify app data and use your own judgment.
